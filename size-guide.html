Size guide

Our tees and hoodies are a regular unisex fit. Measured flat on the garment, then doubled.

How to measure

  • Chest: lay a tee that fits you well flat. Measure armpit to armpit and double it.
  • Length: from the top of the shoulder to the hem.
  • Between sizes? Size up for a relaxed fit, down for a closer fit.

Hoodies

Hoodies are cut relaxed. Take your usual tee size.

Caps

One size with an adjustable strap.
