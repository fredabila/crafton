Our story

Code, create and wear your passion.

Craf7ton is tech apparel for developers, builders and campus tech clubs. We make the clothes we wanted to wear to class, to standup and to the hackathon: clean on the front, loud on the back.

Made for builders

Developers shop by identity, not by garment type. So we design by stack: frontend, backend, DevOps, AI and data, hardware. If you know, you know.

Made with clubs

We work with student tech communities to make merch that people actually wear after the event. Your logo, your colours, our fabric.

Spelled with a 7

Craf7ton. The 7 is on purpose.
