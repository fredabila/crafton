Delivery and returns

Delivery

  • Campus pickup: free at UMaT and partner campuses. We message you on WhatsApp or SMS when it's ready.
  • Courier: anywhere in Ghana, usually 1 to 3 working days after dispatch.
  • Free delivery on orders of 10 pieces or more.
  • You'll get your order confirmation by WhatsApp or SMS, and by email if you gave one.

Returns and exchanges

  • Unworn, unwashed items with tags can be exchanged or returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • Message us on WhatsApp with your order reference to start a return.
  • Custom club orders are made to order and can't be returned, unless there's a printing fault.
  • Faulty item? Send a photo and we'll replace it.

Payments

Mobile Money (MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo) and cards.
